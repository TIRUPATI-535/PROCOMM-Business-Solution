Our History

From a Pune office to a trusted GeM partner

Established in 2016, our company is located in the heart of Pune City. Over the years, we have grown into a reputable firm, offering an extensive range of products and services tailored to the needs of the commercial sector. Our commitment to excellence is reflected in our unwavering dedication to tackling challenges with a proactive and healthy approach. We prioritize individual attention, ensuring that each client receives personalized solutions.

In addition to our commercial ventures, we have made significant strides in the government sector. As a trusted supplier, we provide high-quality products and services to various government agencies. We assist buyers and sellers in navigating the GeM and Maha E-Tender platforms, ensuring a smooth and efficient process.
